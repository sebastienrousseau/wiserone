# Governance

wiserone is maintained by Sebastien Rousseau. Decisions are made in the
open through GitHub issues and pull requests, guided by correctness of the
daily rotation, output stability, security, and long-term maintainability.

The maintainer has final responsibility for releases, security response,
the corpus mirror, and project scope. Decisions that will be questioned
later are recorded as architecture decision records under `docs/adr/`.
Contributors may appeal a decision by opening a focused issue with new
technical evidence. The project's direction is in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

The corpus follows the website: changes to the quote pool land on
wiserone.com first and are mirrored here (see
[`docs/POLICIES.md`](docs/POLICIES.md#corpus-changes)).

Inactive ownership is addressed by transferring repository and crates.io
ownership to a trusted contributor before any planned departure. The
security contact is kept current in [`SECURITY.md`](.github/SECURITY.md).
