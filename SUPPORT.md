# Support

- Usage questions and reproducible defects: open a
  [GitHub issue](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/wiserone/issues).
  Include the command you ran, the corpus file, and the output.
- Documentation: start with the [README](README.md),
  [`docs/USER-GUIDE.md`](docs/USER-GUIDE.md) and the
  [API reference](https://docs.rs/wiserone).
- Vulnerabilities: follow [SECURITY.md](.github/SECURITY.md); do not open
  a public issue.

Community support is best-effort and carries no response-time guarantee.
