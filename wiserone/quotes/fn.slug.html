Builds the URL slug wiserone.com publishes a quote under.

The site’s canonical page for every quote is /q/<slug>/, and each dated URL points its rel=canonical there, so this is the only truthful canonical for a generated page.

Lowercases, strips anything outside [a-z0-9] to single hyphens, and truncates to 64 characters on a hyphen boundary — the same rule the site’s generator applies. Kept in step by test_slug_matches_published_urls.
