## Highlights ⭐️

* **`wiserone all` scales linearly**: It refreshes the log, `index.html` and `sitemap.xml` once per run instead of after every page, so 1,000 quotes take about 1.5 s instead of 37 s, and the bundled 136 in about a tenth of a second. The pages, index, sitemap and logged events it writes are unchanged.
* **Batch page generation in the library**: `html::write_html_files` writes many pages without the per-page refresh, and `html::refresh_output_index` performs that refresh once.
